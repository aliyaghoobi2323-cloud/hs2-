## verdict
NOT accepted as a default: the key stays OFF (lab key).
- dark download with the key: 4/7 (+n1) survive vs 0/7 off — a big gain but not a fix; the 3 failures are one
  deterministic-looking deadlock (resend queued behind a full window while Iran's reorder room is full).
- one side new does not help a download (m1/m2 froze): the receiver must be new too.
- quiet u8 shows a possible throughput/p99 cost (n=3/4), and false closes are ~0.5/run, not 0.
Next (in order): (1) receiver: let the sub carrying the resend be read past the room — e.g. per-sub room equal to the
  sub's smux window while a hole is old, or the exit resends the hole chunk on a FRESH sub (nothing queued ahead); or
  sender: stop striping new chunks while a stall-resend is unacked. (2) frozen path to max(1 s, 8 x srtt).
  (3) u8 A/B n>=6 with CPU per side. (4) idle -race ./engine.
