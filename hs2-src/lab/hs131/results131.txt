## candidate fix (rel63 470c4fb6, binary hs2-k131 = rel63 tree + fix): gapRoomAfter 500 ms -> 1.5 s
The hole rooms (gap room, gap fill, hole sub room) and the gapCheck wake now wait 1.5 s of no progress.
A resend cannot arrive earlier: ack-stall close needs ackStallAfter 800 ms + TCP frozen max(1 s, 8 x srtt).
So quiet lossy stalls (< 1.5 s) no longer open rooms; dark recovery keeps them (to be proven by j3 dark runs).
