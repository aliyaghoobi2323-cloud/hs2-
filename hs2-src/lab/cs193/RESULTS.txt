Mechanism (both sides, each its own sending direction):
 - timer (200 ms, HS2_MCONN_DARKCALL_TICK_MS) over every records conn that is call-classified (callOn or
   call-like within callFreeTTL) - covers one-copy and two-copy (call pair) sends and idle senders;
 - per live sub: TCP_INFO (bytes_acked, unacked, backoff, total_retrans) + the link meter's lastRd. DARK =
   unacked > 0 AND no ack progress >= 1 s AND (backoff >= 2 OR >= 2 retransmits since the last ack progress)
   AND nothing received on the link >= 1 s (max(1 s, 4x packet interval): call-like means >= mCallMinRate,
   so the interval term is < 1 s). A lossy-but-alive link still receives every RTT -> never dark;
 - own primary DARK -> switch to a live sub on another socket AND another path that is not dark, heard
   within 1 s and acking (or idle); resend smux written-consumed from the records ring, move its queue
   (cs189's switch). The peer sees the same dark link and switches its own primary;
 - no candidate: Iran starts an er97 records rescue bypassing the path-level silent set (re-arm after 3 s);
   Iran also when a live sub is dark and its receive has not advanced for 1 s;
 - in-flight cap: a call sub with written-consumed > max(256 KB, 3/4 of the ring) takes no chunk until the
   next visit (stale cap > 1 s ignored), so the ring covers the resend;
 - <= 1 action per 3 s per conn; counters (10 s "mconn darkcall:" line) + per-action log lines + outcome
   (receive or new-primary ack progress within 2 s).
