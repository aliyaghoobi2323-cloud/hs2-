Code (Iran = the edge: every stripe growth is decided there, the exit only follows; no wire change):
HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_DL_FAST (engine/mconn_stripe_dlfast.go)
  - raw connection's first 3 s: own download sampler every 200 ms; 2 samples in a row at >= 80 Mbit/s
    (HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_DL_FAST_MBIT) received (more than sent) -> raw->stripe upgrade at once
  - after 3 s: 2 hot 1 s ticks at >= 80 Mbit/s instead of stripeHotTicks (4)
  - doubling chain (stFastMore) for the download while every sub carries 30 Mbit/s, capped by K;
    each sub through the same picker (share/keep-free, light links never taken); 1 s ticks keep the
    queue gate (a sub unused -> no growth)
  - solo: 1 fast download at a time (HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_DL_FAST_MAX); d8 others grow the old way
  - dr116b: a fast download's failed stripe open retries at 250-500 ms (as a fast upload) instead of 2 s
  - one log line per fast download ("download stripe at 1.6s (285 Mbit/s, chain 1)")
Default: ON at the edge via the envdef production table (HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_DL_FAST=0 turns it off).
