## verdict
- The unbounded growth is fixed: a rel63(hs133) side next to a rel61 side no longer opens hole rooms (room_old_peer
  counts the refusals) — dark download edge RSS max 630-675 MB (hs132: 2672 MB), dark upload exit max 840 MB, both
  falling back after the blackhole; the mixed pair behaves like rel61 in the dark (download dies; upload with the new
  side as SENDER survives: uB).
- Caps: 64 MiB/conn is too small (dA: both-new dark download died, room_capped 976). Defaults 256 MiB/conn,
  512 MiB/process: both-new dark down 2/2 + up 1/1 survive, 128/256 1/1 + 1/1; edge RSS max 600-947 MB (hs132 A 885).
- Quiet: hs133 is inert in quiet runs (rooms 0); key ON vs off d8 call99/crowd99 t +1.2/+1.3 (n=4 vs 3) — same sign
  as hs132, not significant; open item for a pooled look.
- Rollout: bin63b replaces bin63 (do not deploy bin63). Order is now free memory-wise; for dark survival both sides
  must be on it. Recommended: kharej (exit) first, then Iran (edge) — exit-first keeps dark uploads like uC (old edge,
  new exit) dying as rel61 did with bounded RSS, and edge-first (uB/dB) is also bounded. No env change needed;
  HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_KEEPSTALL=0 stays the rollback.
