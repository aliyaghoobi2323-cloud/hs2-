== (c) Crowd failures on blackhole / RST / flap — analysis (lbv81 s1-lab2 samples.txt, F lines by 2 s
   bin from event start)
  blackhole e (600 s): +8:45 +10:52 +12:29 +14:21 +16:5 +18:9 +20:3 (164) — nothing before +8 s.
  RST g (1110 s):       +0:34 +2:22 +4:20 +6:20 +8:1 +10:11 +12:37 — RSTs kill raw/single conns at once.
  flap f (810-1050 s):  +8:66 +10:61 +12:31 +14:4 ... same shape repeated every blackhole half-cycle.
The probe's crowd request timeout is ~8 s: the +8..+12 s bins are requests ALREADY IN FLIGHT on the
dead path at t=0 on conns without a good copy (raw / single subs: lbv81 census "raw 5541, single 120,
dual all-marked 124" conn-seconds); the +12..+22 s tail is conns PLACED on the dead path's links after
the blackhole, until the per-link suspect check (suspectAfter 12 s, tick 2 s) stops new placement.
So a faster placement stop can only save the tail; the in-flight bulk needs a copy on another path
before ~8 s (MCONN for those conns), not a faster judge.
