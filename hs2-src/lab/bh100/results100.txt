bh100 — HS2_LB_SILENT: why a blackholed path did not come back after the clear, and the fix. Base bh93/bh98 4fd15d87.
Lab1 only (198.51.100.168), labslot single slots (soak slot 0, A/B 2 workers). Runs T=260 s: blackhole 25..145 s, 115 s clean.
Runner d100.sh/seq100.sh, summary sum100.py; lab runner patch run86b-direction.diff (rK = Iran->kharej only, xK = both).
Scenarios: bI Iran IP 52.1 (all kharej IPs) kh->ir dropped, bP path 49.2->53.1 kh->ir dropped, rI/rP ir->kh only, xI/xP both.

DIAGNOSIS (g1bIS1/g1bPS1, bh98 build + "lb silentdbg" per-path counts under HS2_LB_REC_DEBUG):
 1. The blackholed path's dials time out -> resultLocked COOLS it ~5-40 s after the cut. A cooled path is not eligible:
    adapt2 drops its readings and judge.forget() deletes its judge state (so no "recovering" line can ever be logged;
    bh98's "rec = -" was partly a measurement artefact). Its links die meanwhile (held 0 at the clear).
 2. After the clear the only way back is the cooled path's probe dial, one per lbProbeEvery: 2 min in production,
    20 s in the lab (HS2_LB_FAST). Lab: back +22 s (bI) / +32 s (bP); S0 the same mechanism (+22 / +52 s, or > 115 s).
 3. Even then the path stayed at its cut factor 0.10 for good: it has no judge entry until it gives a reading, and
    its 2 probe-floor links never carry enough segments for one (carry still < lb2MinSegs/4 after a minute), so
    adapt2 kept target = old mult. (a1bIS1/a1bPS1: 2 links of ~20-28 at clear+110 s.)
 4. A due probe waits for the pool's next dial; a steady pool makes none (b2bIS1: both paths back only +22.8 s).
 Not the cause: old links stuck in RTO backoff (they all died during the 120 s; held 0 at the clear).

FIX (all under HS2_LB_SILENT, key off = old behaviour; engine/lb_silent.go, lb.go, lb_auto2.go, lb_spare.go):
 a. silentCooldown: a path cooled for dials that failed before TCP connected (dialConnectStage: no SYN-ACK) is
    probed every lbSilentProbe = 2 s instead of lbProbeEvery. One SYN per probe; only a two-way path answers, so it
    stays out while dark in any direction. TLS-stage failures keep lbProbeEvery.
 b. silentBackTarget: a kept path with no judge entry (back from a cooldown) gets target 1 -> its share comes back
    step by step (lbMaxStepPct a reading); the judge lowers/cuts it again on its first bad reading.
 c. silentProbeDueLocked: fillMin asks the pool for one spare dial when a cooled path's probe is due and not in
    flight (lb2SpareMax bounds the surplus).
 unit tests: TestSilentProbeDarkPathFast (on dark 2 s / off / tls; held out over 3 failed probes; back on connect),
   TestSilentBackFromCooldownRegainsShare (on: 0.10 -> 1.0 step-limited; off: stays 0.10),
   TestSilentProbeDueAsksSpare (only after the cooldown, not while in flight, never key off).
   go test ./engine -run "Silent|LB2|LBPeerOutage|LBPathDeath|LBOldPeer|Q2|Spare|FillMin|CutDrain" ok (lab1).
 debug: "lb silentdbg" per-path line each quality tick (only with HS2_LB_REC_DEBUG=1 and the key).
