== Change (engine/mconn_rescue_rec.go, HS2_MCONN_RESCUE_REC, on whenever HS2_MCONN_RESCUE=1 unless =0)
  Records 0x10 RREC(Bi=sendOff, Ri=recv next) Iran→exit, 0x11 ACK(Bk, Rk), 0x12 NAK(Bk, Rk); capExt bit9.
  - Both sides of a role-3 conn keep, once in records mode, a ring of the DATA bytes they wrote, by offset
    (rescueRing with rec=true: 32 KB/conn HS2_MCONN_RESCUE_REC_KB, own budget 48 MB HS2_MCONN_RESCUE_REC_MB,
    separate from the raw rings' 64 MB). Idle 10 s: freed (restarts empty at its end offset); kept while a
    rescue waits; freed at conn end. Over budget: restarts empty (counted gap), never stops for good.
  - Iran (same silent-set trigger and per-second bound as RESCUE): a conn whose EVERY live sub sits on the
    silent set opens a join off the silent path, retires+removes the old subs (rrAband: their death ends
    nothing, their queues are released), snapshots its ring below Bi, sends RREC first on the join.
  - Exit: ring holds [Ri, Bk) -> retires every other sub, ACK + resend [Ri, Bk) (+FIN if sent); else NAK,
    keeps its subs, the join becomes primary (conn NOT ended). Iran resends [Rk, Bi) from the snapshot on
    ACK or NAK (+FIN); snapshot short of Rk -> conn ends (nocover = old outcome). Reassembly dedupes by offset.
  - Only toward an exit announcing bit9 (Iran checks at the role-3 open); a peer without it never sees 0x10-12.
  Unit tests (mconn_rescue_rec_test.go): LostOnlySub (both dirs blackholed, req+resp in flight, delivered
  once, conn goes on, rings freed+unregistered at close), DualOneDeadNoRescue, NakKeepsConn, LiveNoDuplicate,
  MixedCapability. TestRescue* -count=3 ok, TestRescueRec -race -count=5 ok.
