== (b) EARLY RESCUE on blackholed paths — NOT implemented; see EARLY-RESCUE-DESIGN.txt.
A copy opened at +1.5 s carries only bytes written after it opens: raw first subs keep no buffer and their
upgrade must finish over the dead sub; records-mode light conns keep no sent chunks (keepSentLocked only
for striping conns, m.stN > 0). The in-flight requests that make the +8..+12 s failure bulk cannot be
saved by a copy; it needs a resend (ring of recent raw bytes / light keep buffer + a RESCUE handshake
that does not wait on the dead sub, capability bit). Out of this job's time.
