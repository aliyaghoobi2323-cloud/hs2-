cs141 — why a single test download sometimes never stripes at night, and HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_DL_FAIR (default off)

Lab: lab2, d139 lab (10 paths 45 ms, loss 0.2-2 %, crowd 3000 + call + game), edge rel62k2 (prod 5d95d5c7) vs cs141,
exit rel61h2 (4a3abdf9) both. 3-10 background iperf3 users at 20-200 Mbit/s (mostly downloads, some uploads) from t=0,

Repro (K = production edge): the test download is bimodal exactly as in production — 107-217 Mbit/s flat in the
crowded sets, 700+ only when few background users. Two causes, both in the logs:
 1. DL_FAST solo hog: the first background download >= 80 Mbit/s took the fast slot at ~1.5 s and held it for its
    whole life (stripeDlActive is released only when its last stripe sub dies) -> one "download stripe at" per run
    (prod: one for four downloads); every later download needs 4 hot 1 s ticks + the raw handshake (~5 s).
 2. Stripe slot cap: stripeMaxConns = 8 per process. Background streams of only 20-50 Mbit/s pass the growth rule
    (>= 5 Mbit/s per sub) and stripe to 2-8 subs, so all 8 slots are taken; the test download is refused on every
    tick (stripeStat.refused) and stays on ONE link for its whole life (r1K: 8 bg conns striped, the test never logged
    "striped over 2"). This is the "never stripes at all" mode (329/380 in prod).

Fix (engine/mconn_stripe_dlfair.go, key HS2_MCONN_STRIPE_DL_FAIR=1, off by default, no wire change, edge only):
 - the fast slot is a 3 s lease (kept while its doubling chain/open is pending), then given back (subs kept);
 - only a download's first growth (n == 1) takes the slot; a striped background download never re-takes it
   (solo kept: still 1 fast start at a time — rel54b lesson);
 - a download at >= stripeDlFastRate (80 Mbit/s) may take one of 4 stripe slots past stripeMaxConns (first growth only;
   uploads and slow streams never).
Arm F = lease/first-growth only (j1), arm X = final (j2).
